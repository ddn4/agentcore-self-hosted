# Return policy (team Support)

1. **Damaged or defective items** are always eligible for a full refund if the
   customer reports the damage within **30 days** of delivery. No return
   shipment is required for items under $100.
2. **Change of mind** returns are eligible within 30 days of delivery if the
   item is unused and in its original packaging.
3. Requests made **more than 30 days** after delivery are **not eligible** for a
   refund. Offer the manufacturer's warranty instead.
4. Support agents **cannot issue refunds**. Refunds are approved and paid by the
   Finance team.
5. **Refunds over $100** need a **Finance manager's approval**. Tell the
   customer the request has been escalated for approval and that Finance will
   confirm the refund.
